## Recording notes

- The three-door overview and full opening-to-mascot sequence are required, not optional B-roll. Capture on desktop with animations enabled for the recording; do not alter the app’s reduced-motion support. Wait for the actual sequence to finish before framing the recommendation. If it needs extra time, shorten the final partnership mention rather than the animation.
- Use a successful live journey with all three returned directions for this shot. If a request returns fewer results, try another supported input; never invent or duplicate a door to fill the frame.
- Target three minutes with a slightly brisker delivery around 155 words per minute. Spend the time saved on extra control close-ups, not an accelerated opening animation. Rehearse each segment and leave the closing line unhurried.
- Extra screen coverage: personal-consideration categories; intention/depth; Under the Door evidence; editable group members; current/saved/new What If starts; scenario overrides; guest-link customization; Lab Keep/favorite/restore; planner duration/reorder/checkoff; illustrated story sharing; preference restore/forget; reward tiers; optional sound and mobile layout.
- These are short feature highlights, not demonstrations of every action. Keep text legible and show one focal area at a time. If the rehearsal overruns, cut secondary control close-ups before cutting any required feature or the complete door animation.
- Required coverage checklist: both avenues; all four exploration options; three-door overview and opening animation; Qloo’s role; Discovery Lab; practical tools; My Doors; badges and saved progress; Trust. Do not trade away any of these for partnership coverage.
- Capture real successful Qloo responses. Never present fixtures or prepared examples as live Qloo output. Do not promise particular result titles: show whatever the recorded request actually returns.
- Waiting can be shortened with a visible cut or “request time shortened” caption. Keep provenance visible; a stored response is not a newly executed request.
- If microphone capture is unavailable, show typing. A scripted voice-input preview must be labeled as a preview, not live recognition.
- For What If?, demonstrate depth or city as a retrieval-changing condition. A budget-only edit may return identical entities; do not manufacture a difference.
- Debate is rule-based, not unrestricted AI chat. Show a recognized challenge and an actual alternative request.
- Use an available physical result for the coordinate map action. Otherwise show a supplied website or the planner’s explicitly labeled category search; do not imply a musician or film is a nearby venue.
- Hidden gems are authored activities, not additional Qloo facts. Availability, opening hours, prices, travel time, and accessibility are not verified.
- Capture saved progress with a real authorized account before assembling the final video. Show actual earned or locked rewards; do not fabricate badges or imply real-world achievements. If authorized capture is unavailable, keep the narration but label a supplied-art preview as illustrative—not a live signed-in screen—and disclose the limitation.
- Distinguish account-backed saved doors, reward progress, and optional four-setting memory from browser-local Lab history and session-only conversations. Do not claim every activity syncs across devices.
- Preserve the established animated mascots. PDF or still captures may use their existing still variants.
- Partnerships are omitted from the timed narration to protect core feature coverage. Only if a rehearsal leaves room, add: “There’s a future partnership model, too.” Do not replace Help others explore with an advertising tour. Sponsorships, referrals, and external API/MCP access are future opportunities or labeled mockups—not live commercial integrations.
